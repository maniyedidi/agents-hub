NIMBUS CLOUD - EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)
Version 3.2 - Effective 1 January 2026

1. ABOUT NIMBUS CLOUD
Nimbus Cloud was founded in 2014 by Priya Raman and Daniel Ortiz in Pune, India.
The company builds backup and storage software for small businesses.
Headquarters: 14 Lakeview Road, Pune. Second office: Austin, Texas.
Nimbus has 240 employees. The CEO is Priya Raman. The CTO is Daniel Ortiz.

2. WORKING HOURS AND REMOTE WORK
Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday.
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Fully remote work needs written approval from a department head.
Office days are Tuesday and Thursday for the Pune team.

3. LEAVE POLICY
Annual leave: 24 paid days per year. Up to 8 unused days can be carried over.
Sick leave: 12 paid days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days.
Parental leave: 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the other parent.
Public holidays follow the local office calendar.
Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance.

4. EXPENSES AND TRAVEL
Meals while travelling: up to 1,500 INR (or 25 USD) per day.
Flights must be booked at least 14 days ahead in economy class.
Hotel limit: 9,000 INR (or 130 USD) per night.
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days with receipts attached.
Claims above 50,000 INR need approval from the Finance Director, Meera Joshi.

5. SECURITY RULES
Passwords must have at least 14 characters and be changed every 180 days.
Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts.
Lost laptops must be reported to security@nimbus.example within 2 hours.
Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.

6. SUPPORT AND ESCALATION
Severity 1 incidents (service down): respond within 15 minutes, 24x7.
Severity 2 incidents (major feature broken): respond within 2 hours.
Severity 3 incidents (minor issue): respond within 1 business day.
The on-call engineer rotation changes every Monday at 09:00.
The incident commander for Severity 1 is always the engineering manager on duty.

7. LEARNING BUDGET
Each employee gets 60,000 INR per year for courses, books and conferences.
Unused budget does not carry over. Manager approval is required above 20,000 INR.

HR: hr@nimbus.example, extension 210.
IT helpdesk: it@nimbus.example, extension 305.
Finance: finance@nimbus.example, extension 120.
